Evaluate a coapplicative expression, exiting on the first Left if it exists.

>>> coeval $ Left "foo" :| [Right "bar"]
Left "foo" :| []
>>> coeval $ Right "foo" :| [Right "bar"]
Right "foo" :| [Right "bar"]

apS :: Selective f => f (a -> b) -> f a -> f b Source #

Recover the application operator <*> from <*?. Rigid selective functors satisfy the law <*> = apS and furthermore, the resulting applicative functor satisfies all laws of Applicative:

  • Identity:

    pure id <*> v = v

  • Homomorphism:

    pure f <*> pure x = pure (f x)

  • Interchange:

    u <*> pure y = pure ($y) <*> u

  • Composition:

    (.) <$> u <*> v <*> w = u <*> (v <*> w)

Accumulate the Right values, or return the first Left.
